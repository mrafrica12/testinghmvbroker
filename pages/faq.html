General

What does an independent insurance broker do? +

An independent broker shops your coverage across multiple insurance carriers instead of selling policies from just one company. HMV Brokers compares options from several carriers to find the plan that fits your health, budget, and coverage goals, rather than pushing a single insurer's product.

Do independent brokers cost more than going straight to an insurance company? +

No. Carriers build broker commissions into their standard rates, so working with an independent broker like HMV Brokers typically costs the same as buying directly from the insurer — with the added benefit of a licensed agent comparing multiple carriers on your behalf at no extra cost to you.

Health Insurance

What's the difference between an HMO and a PPO? +

An HMO requires you to use an in-network primary care doctor and get referrals for specialists, usually at a lower cost. A PPO costs more but lets you see any provider, in or out of network, without a referral.

When can I enroll in individual or family health insurance? +

Open enrollment runs once a year, but qualifying life events — losing employer coverage, marriage, a new baby, moving — open a special enrollment window. HMV Brokers can tell you which window applies to your situation.

Life Insurance

How much life insurance do I actually need? +

A common starting point is 10–12x your annual income, adjusted for debts, a mortgage, and future costs like college. HMV Brokers runs the specific numbers for your household rather than applying a flat rule.

Can life insurance be part of my retirement plan? +

Yes — permanent life insurance builds cash value you can access tax-advantaged in retirement, which is why some clients use it alongside, not instead of, traditional retirement accounts.

Retirement & Income Planning

When should I start planning for retirement? +

As early as possible — but the strategy that matters most is the one for whatever stage you're in now, whether that's 10 years out, 5 years out, or already retired.

How is retirement planning different from a 401(k)? +

A 401(k) is one savings vehicle. Retirement planning is the broader strategy — how you'll turn savings, Social Security, and other income sources into dependable income once paychecks stop.

What is income planning? +

It's the strategy for converting retirement savings into a steady stream of income — deciding what to withdraw, when, and from which accounts, so your money lasts as long as you need it to.

Long-Term Care & Disability

What does long-term care insurance actually cover? +

It covers costs standard health insurance and Medicare largely don't — nursing home care, assisted living, and in-home care for help with daily activities like bathing or dressing.

What does disability income insurance cover? +

It replaces a portion of your income — typically 50–65% — if illness or injury keeps you from working. Policies vary on how "disabled" is defined, which HMV Brokers walks through before you buy.

Is my employer's disability coverage enough? +

Often not on its own. Group coverage is a good baseline, but payout caps and the fact that benefits are usually taxable mean many people layer an individual policy on top.

Commercial, Auto & Group

What does general liability insurance cover for a business? +

It covers third-party bodily injury, property damage, and related legal costs if your business is found responsible — the baseline policy most commercial leases and contracts require.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage? +

No — standard homeowners policies exclude flood damage. Flood coverage requires a separate policy, typically through the National Flood Insurance Program.

What's included in a typical group benefits package? +

Most commonly group health insurance, plus optional ancillary lines like dental, vision, life, and disability — bundled to give employees meaningful coverage at group rates.
