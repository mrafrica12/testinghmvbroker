About HMV Brokers

Founder-Led, Independent, Since 2013

HMV Brokers gives Metro Atlanta individuals, families, and businesses one relationship for health, life, retirement, and business insurance — backed by 20+ licensed agents and direct access to leadership.

Our Mission

Customized strategies, not one-size-fits-all quotes

HMV Brokers serves individuals, families, and businesses across every area of insurance planning. Every strategy focuses on safety, protection, and good planning — covering health insurance, property & casualty insurance, life insurance, retirement planning, Medicare, and more.

Our Team

24+ combined years of experience

Led by a founder who has worked in insurance since 2004, and an office manager who keeps daily operations — and clients — on track.

Humphrey Wedey has been an insurance professional since 2004. He started as an agent for Delta Life Insurance Co, then joined AIG American General as a worksite sales agent, before becoming an independent agent in 2008. In January 2013, Humphrey opened HMV Brokers, specializing in health and life insurance — the agency has since grown to 20+ agents, licensed in personal and commercial lines as well as health and life. Humphrey is an immigrant from Ghana who has made Atlanta his home; he's married with three children, a devoted Liverpool FC fan, and spends his free time with family at the movies or the park.
