Humphrey Wedey

Insurance Broker

Humphrey Wedey has been an insurance professional since 2004, Humphrey started as agent for Delta Life Insurance Co then later joined AIG American general as a worksite sales agent before becoming an independent agent in 2008.


In January of 2013 Humphrey opened his agency HMV Brokers, Inc specializing in health and life insurance. The agency has grown over the past 5 past years to have over 20 agents working under the agency. Humphrey and some of his agents are licensed in personal and commercial lines as well as health and life.


Humphrey is an immigrant from Ghana but has called Atlanta his home for the past 18 years, he is married with 3 children. He is a big soccer fan with Liverpool FC his favorite team, besides soccer he likes basketball as well. In his free time, he is usually out with his family either at the movies or at the parks.
